# XP and humanoid refinement — 2026-09-10

- Successful general skill actions no longer hit a permanent zero-XP cliff. Over-levelled actions retain a 5% practice floor with fractional carry, so tiny actions must accumulate before one whole XP is awarded.
- Overall level progression remains server-owned and derives from credited skill training. The HUD exposes current level progress as a thin bottom-edge bar.
- Skill XP gains create up to three compact progress bars for the affected skills. Repeated gains aggregate; each bar disappears 2.6 seconds after its latest gain. Initial snapshots and reconnects do not manufacture gain popups.
- The humanoid rig uses a smaller head, longer legs, narrower limbs and full trouser legs. Face clusters add jaw and ear structure. Body, hair and worn equipment continue to use the same 64×64 rig and foot anchor.
- No third-party sprite set was imported. Keeping the deterministic Atelier source avoids licensing ambiguity and preserves complete equipment-layer coverage.
